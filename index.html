STILL UNSURE? ASK HERE ↗
How long can I book a driving lesson for? +

You can enquire about a 60-minute, 90-minute, 120-minute or four-hour lesson. The instructor will confirm prices, available durations and whether a longer session is appropriate.

Can I start if I've never driven before? +

Yes. Request a first-time driving lesson and share your level of experience so the instructor can plan an appropriate starting point.

Can I request automatic or manual driving? +

Choose a transmission in the enquiry form. The business will need to confirm current vehicle and instructor availability before making a booking.

Do you offer parking or roundabout practice? +

You can request focused practice with parking, manoeuvres, intersections and other situations you find difficult. Include your goals in the notes.

Can you help me prepare for the NSW driving test? +

Test preparation can focus on observation, safe decisions and everyday driving skills. Official test eligibility and bookings remain subject to NSW government requirements.

Does sending an enquiry reserve a lesson? +

No. This website demonstration helps you put together a lesson enquiry. A confirmed reservation requires an actual response from the business.

Do instructor lessons count towards NSW learner logbook hours? +

Eligible structured lessons from appropriately licensed instructors can qualify for additional logbook credit under NSW rules. Ask the instructor and refer to current Transport for NSW conditions.
